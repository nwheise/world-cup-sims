How this works
  • Model: goals are Poisson draws scaled by an Elo expectation on FIFA's 600-point scale (avg 2.7 goals/match); knockout ties break with a small skill edge as an extra-time/penalties proxy. Ratings are the April 2026 FIFA points (estimates for unranked teams). No host advantage, no injuries.
  • Rules: official 2026 regulations end to end — head-to-head-first group tiebreakers, ranking of third-placed teams, and FIFA's real Annex C table (all 495 combinations) routing the best 8 thirds into the bracket.
  • Live results: actual scores are merged in as the tournament is played — refreshed about every hour from ESPN's public scoreboard (with the public-domain openfootball dataset as a fallback). Played games are pinned as fact; the simulation only fills in what hasn't happened yet, so every probability sharpens as the tournament goes.
  • Your preferences: teams start ranked by strength (the default assumption: you want to see the best teams); head-to-head picks personalize from there, and ⭐ pinned teams are locked above everything else. "Lineup quality" is the favorite-weighted appeal of the distinct teams you'd watch across your matches.
  • Cheer guide: one big simulation records every game's outcome and who ends up in your attended matches; bucketing by outcome gives the causal effect of each result on your expected lineup quality. A loyalty guard stops it from telling you to root against a team you love unless the payoff is real (for that team or another favorite) — and it will never tell you to cheer against a team you've pinned.
  • Privacy: everything runs in your browser; your picks live in localStorage and never leave your device.
